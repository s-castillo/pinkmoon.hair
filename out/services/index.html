Services

What I Offer

Our wellness salon offers more than hair styling — we are focused on bringing wholeness to our clients through self-reflection. Our salon offers an environment for clients to enjoy, relax, and be present. We cultivate a safe space for self-care, self-expression, and positive change. I believe the beauty and wellness experience should leave you with more than a service you love — it should leave you feeling uplifted, confident, and reconnected to the best version of yourself. With over a decade of experience as a Texas certified cosmetologist, I bring a thoughtful balance of technical precision and intuitive artistry to every appointment. As a practicing tarot consultant and astrological chart interpreter, I offer one-on-one guidance and care in my home studio. Here at Pink Moon we offer a diverse range of self-care through creating looks, intuitive guidance, and sensory touch services.
